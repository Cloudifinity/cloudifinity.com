What we do

Cloud, on-premises, or a blend of both. We put each part of your infrastructure where it actually makes sense, build it properly, and keep it that way.

The whole picture

Infrastructure, wherever it runs

No dogma about cloud versus on-premises. We design and build the whole picture and put each workload where it fits your data, your costs and your operations, from a cloud platform to a server in your building, joined up and kept running. One practice with deep cloud and security engineering alongside real networking and hardware experience.

Cloud & AWS platforms

Architecture, migrations and platform builds on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Infrastructure as code with Terraform, container platforms with Kubernetes, and cloud cost kept firmly under control. Deep cloud and security engineering is a core strength.

Servers, on-prem & networks

Servers, storage and on-prem systems specified, deployed and maintained, plus the networks, WiFi and structured cabling underneath. The reliable, hands-on foundation, built properly and easy to live with.

Monitored & kept running

We keep an eye on it so you do not have to. Monitoring, regular checks and hands-on support, cloud or on-site, with problems spotted and dealt with before they reach you.

A specialism

Venues & events

Event spaces have a harder version of the same problem: everything has to hold up on a full-house night, with no second chances. It is where reliable infrastructure stops being a nice-to-have, and it is work we are especially built for.

  • WiFi engineered to handle hundreds or thousands of devices at once, without the dead spots
  • Segmented networks so card payments and ticketing stay fast and protected even when guest WiFi is busy
  • Event-day readiness ahead of your key dates, with cover arranged around your calendar
  • A local point of contact who knows your venue

Kept running, not just installed

Once it is in, we do not disappear. You get monitoring, regular checks and someone to call when you need them, cloud or on-site. Support is 9 to 5, Monday to Friday, and for venues we arrange cover around your event calendar so the nights that matter are looked after.

  • Proactive monitoring, so faults are caught early
  • Scheduled checks and tidy-ups to stop small issues becoming big ones
  • A point of contact who knows your setup
  • Event-day cover for venues, arranged around your calendar
