What we do

The unglamorous infrastructure that decides whether an event runs smoothly or falls over. We build it properly, then keep it that way.

What we do best

Networks & WiFi that survive event day

Coverage across the whole venue, with WiFi engineered to handle hundreds or thousands of devices at once instead of falling over at the worst possible moment. We separate the networks that matter, so card payments and ticketing stay fast and protected even when the guest WiFi is busy, and we design out the dead spots before they become someone's problem on the night.

Structured cabling & hardware

Cabling, switches, access points and racks installed tidily and labelled properly, so when something needs attention it is quick to find and quick to fix, not an afternoon of guesswork.

Connectivity you can rely on

Internet uplinks with failover, so one line going down does not stop the show. Guest and staff networks kept sensibly separate, and enough headroom for the days when the building is full.

Monitored & kept running

We keep an eye on it so you do not have to. Monitoring, monthly checks and event-day readiness, with problems spotted and dealt with before they reach your guests.

Kept running, not just installed

Once it is in, we do not disappear. You get monitoring, regular checks and someone local to call when you need them. Support is 9 to 5, Monday to Friday, with cover for your big dates arranged around your event calendar, so the nights that matter are looked after.

  • Scheduled checks and tidy-ups to stop small issues becoming big ones
  • Event-day readiness ahead of your key dates
  • A local point of contact who knows your venue
