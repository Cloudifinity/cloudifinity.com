A hands-on infrastructure partner

Cloudifinity is an independent, founder-led infrastructure practice. We bring nearly twenty years of experience across Amazon Web Services (AWS), Kubernetes, Terraform, networking and Linux systems to businesses that need their technology to be dependable, secure and well run.

We work across the full picture, not just one corner of it. That might mean building a platform in the cloud, wiring up reliable networking and WiFi on site, or joining the two together into a hybrid setup that gives you the best of both. Whatever the mix, we build it to a security-first standard, the same standard expected in regulated industries, applied at a scale that suits you.

You deal directly with the person doing the work. No layers, no handoffs, no jargon for its own sake. Just infrastructure that does what it should.
