What we do

Secure AI infrastructure is our core, financial services first. We design, build and run self-hosted AI inference on sensitive data, kept where it has to stay and integrated securely with the cloud, built for the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA), already in force, and the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) high-risk obligations applying from 2 August 2026. Everything else we do, the cloud, the networks and the managed support, exists to make that inference run safely and where it has to stay.

Our core specialism

Secure, sovereign AI infrastructure

Self-hosted AI inference on your most sensitive data, running where it has to stay and integrated securely with the cloud. We build to sovereignty, data-in-use protection and audit-readiness as design principles, so what you put in front of a regulator stands up.
